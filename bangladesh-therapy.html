Speech therapy in Bangladesh

Official provider information, regional contacts, and questions to help you find suitable care.

Checked against official pages on 18 November 2026.

These are service leads, not endorsements or confirmed bookings. Call first to confirm a qualified therapist, stuttering experience, ages, Bangla/English support, fees, referral requirements, and current sessions. Regional availability can change; do not travel without confirming. Hospital short codes are for calls within Bangladesh.

Need help finding a qualified professional?

Society of Speech & Language Therapists (SSLT) is a Bangladesh professional body, not an appointment clinic. Ask about professional information and how to check a therapist’s qualifications.

Before booking

  1. Describe the support you want and your age group.
  2. Ask about qualifications and experience with stuttering in your language.
  3. Confirm appointment time, exact location, assessment fee, and follow-up costs.
  4. Ask whether remote sessions or reduced-fee services are available; do not assume they are.
  5. Bring your questions and any previous reports you choose to share.
